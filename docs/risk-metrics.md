# Risk metrics

With at least 30 aligned daily returns, the engine returns annualized volatility (252-day assumption), downside deviation, maximum drawdown, and 95% historical VaR/CVaR. Each result includes period, sampling frequency, assumptions, coverage, and an explanation. Sharpe, Sortino, beta, correlation, tracking error, and benchmark excess return remain unavailable until aligned portfolio and benchmark histories plus a configured risk-free rate are stored; they are never inferred.
